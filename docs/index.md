# Welcome to the Canvas Lecture Site for MEGR 2156/2157

![MEGR 2156-7 Title](MEGR2156title.png)

Welcome to the mechanical engineering design class MEGR 2156/2157! As a sophomore level class, you will be expected to have a strong foundation in mathematics and physics, as well as a basic understanding of mechanical engineering principles. This class builds upon that foundation, you have the opportunity to learn about different design process and how to apply engineering principles to create mechanical systems. By the end of this class, you should have a strong understanding of how to design, analyze, and prototype a component and mechanical systems as well as be well-prepared to move on to more advanced coursework in mechanical engineering. I'm looking forward to working with you all this semester!
