## Agenda

This week you learn about two major tools in engineering design, parametric design and finite element analysis (FEA.) Parametric Design allows one to more easily modify and fork a design using driver and driven factors making the design easier. FEA allows a design engineer to examine and evaluate how a design is affected by different criteria.
