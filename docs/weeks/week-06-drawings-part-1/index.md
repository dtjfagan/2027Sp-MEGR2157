## Agenda

Good engineering drawings are the foundation of mechanical engineering design. They are a way to communicate to others the intent of a design. Exceptional engineers create coherent and articulate engineering drawings to effectively communicate a design. Understanding certain engineering standards (ISO/ASME) is a key to creating clear and precise drawings. This week we will discuss how to start to create effective engineering drawings which build on previous classes.
