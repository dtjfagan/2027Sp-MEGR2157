## Agenda

As we close the semester, you are one step closer to becoming a mechanical engineer. With the degree comes much responsibility for your impact on society. As engineers, our impact on society and the environment, both positive and negative, is incalculable, and it is our responsibility to understand and learn from our work. This week you will explore the darker side of our work by researching an engineering disaster and the lessons learned.
