This week we will be discussing how to do a preliminary design of a gearbox and its components. We are covering the conceptual design of a shaft using torsion stress and material strength, calculating speeds and torques of involute spur gears, and learning to design journal bearings under loading.

- Learning Objectives:
    - Design shafts for strength.
    - Calculate the input and outputs speeds and torques of involute spur gear boxes.
    - Design a journal bearing using the forces from the gear train.
    - Become familiar with the gear nomenclature.
